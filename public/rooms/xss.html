The Hall of Echoes

Deep in this hall sits an Echo Crystal. Whisper to it, and your words appear echoed upon the wall.

A careful crystal would simply show your words. But this one is careless — if you whisper a spell (some code), it will actually cast it. Tricking a website into running your words as code like this is called Cross-Site Scripting (XSS).
